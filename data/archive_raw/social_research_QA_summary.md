# CTC Compass — Social compensation research update (2026-10-29)

## Coverage
- Company master: 150 records (7 added from social compensation research).
- Compensation observations: 126 total (35 added from public social posts / community compensation threads).
- Source register: 111 unique source URLs after deduplication; includes social posts and prior company/tax/government sources.
- Platforms searched: Reddit, LeetCode Discuss, indexed X/Twitter results, and salary-discussion pages.
- Directly useful public post observations mainly came from LeetCode Discuss and Reddit. X/Twitter search did not surface enough verifiable, indexable India CTC-breakdown posts to include responsibly.

## Interpretation rules
1. All structured amounts relate to India-based roles.
2. Self-reported posts are not independently verified. Confidence is medium where the post provides role, location and a component breakdown; low where the post is a compilation, expected offer, old, or ambiguous.
3. `annual_amount_min_inr` / `annual_amount_max_inr` follows the basis in `amount_basis`. It is not automatically fixed salary or recurring cash. Read `evidence_notes` before comparing.
4. One-time joining/relocation bonuses and multi-year equity are not equivalent to recurring base pay. Equity values are market-, FX- and vesting-dependent.
5. Where posts give only a stock-unit count or USD grant without an INR valuation, equity fields may be blank or described in notes.
6. Expected offers (Citi Reddit thread) are explicitly marked as not confirmed. Old posts (e.g., Ford 2021 and Motorq 2021) are historical context, not current campus offers.
7. A Broadcom identity inferred by commenters in one Reddit thread was deliberately excluded from the structured observations because the original author did not identify the company.
8. Public social posts may be edited, deleted, copied or inaccurate. Treat them as directional datapoints, not a statistical salary survey.

## Main research finding
Social posts provide the best available detail for splitting headline CTC into base/fixed pay, variable bonus, joining/retention bonus, employer contributions and equity. However, coverage is uneven: large product/semiconductor firms have many breakdowns; smaller campus employers (including Ford Business Solutions, Ashley Furniture, BNP Paribas and Motorq) have much less independently corroborated public detail. User-provided campus figures remain separately labelled as unverified rather than upgraded to confirmed salary data.
