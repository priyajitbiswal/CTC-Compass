Income Tax Slabs & Rules (Tax Year 2026-27)

Statutory framework under the Income-tax Act, 2025 and Finance Act, 2026.

KEY STATUTORY BENEFIT

The Zero-Tax Threshold: ₹12,75,000

Under Section 156(2) of the Income-tax Act, 2025, salaried individuals whose gross salary is up to ₹12.75 Lakhs pay ₹0 Income Tax after deducting the ₹75,000 standard deduction and claiming the ₹60,000 rebate.

How does Marginal Relief work?

If your taxable income is ₹12,25,000 (just ₹25k above the ₹12L threshold), regular slab tax would be ₹63,750. However, statutory marginal relief ensures your tax before cess cannot exceed the income exceeding ₹12L. Thus, tax is capped at exactly ₹25,000 (+ 4% cess = ₹26,000).

Standard Deduction (₹75,000)

Under Section 19(1) of the Income-tax Act, 2025, salaried individuals get an automatic ₹75,000 standard deduction under the new regime without needing to submit any rent receipts or investment proofs.
